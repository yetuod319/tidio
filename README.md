## What Tidio Actually Does

Picture this: someone lands on your website at 2 AM (because apparently, that's when people decide they absolutely need to buy things). Without Tidio, they'd probably bounce. With Tidio? Your AI agent Lyro jumps in, answers their questions about shipping costs or product specs, and maybe even closes the sale—all while you're catching some sleep.

The platform combines four main things:

**Live Chat** that lets you talk to customers in real-time. Simple stuff, but it works beautifully. The widget sits on your site, visitors click it, and boom—you're connected.

**Lyro AI Agent** handles up to 67% of customer questions automatically. Unlike those frustrating chatbots that just loop you through menus, Lyro actually learns from your support content and has real conversations. It's trained on your FAQs, help docs, whatever you feed it.
